### Wiring defects found and fixed

1. **`PETTY CASH` rows were dropped.** 55 of the 56 vouchers carry
   `Type = "PETTY CASH"`, and the importer only accepted `Payment` /
   `OFFICE EXPENSES` / `Advance`. Rs 270 926 of small expenses never reached the
   app. `importPettyCashSheet()` now treats `PETTY CASH` the same as an office
   voucher.
2. **Payroll sheet rename skipped all payroll.** The sheet is now called
   `Salary`; the importer looked for `Salary Advance` only and reported nothing.
   It now accepts `Salary Advance`, `Salary_Advance` and `Salary`, and an absent
   salary sheet is reported instead of silently skipped.
3. **`Cash_Demon` totals were short by the IC column.** The sheet's own `Amount`
   is `notes + QR/BANK + IC`; the importer computed `notes + QR/BANK` and dropped
   IC (42 of 116 day-closes were short, e.g. 2083-03-23 26 075 → 25 755). The IC
   amount is now included and existing counts are corrected in place.
4. **Identical same-day payments were collapsed.** The Collection and Payment
   importers deduplicated on party + date + amount with a boolean lookup, so a
   second genuine receipt (KHANAL DAIRY 1 700 twice on 2083-06-28, receipts 4915
   and 4927; SANA KISAN 100 000 twice on 2083-05-09) was dropped. Both now count
   the rows: row _n_ is imported only when the sheet holds more copies than the
   register.
5. **Internal-issue invoices were duplicated on re-import.** Sales rows with no
   invoice number become `INT-<date>-<seq>` documents; the sequence shifts when
   the sheet changes, so a re-import inserted a second copy of the same cream /
   wastage issue (3 duplicates created before the fix). Internal issues are now
   matched on date + party and corrected in place.
6. **Rent income was counted twice in total income.** The Monthly Report added
   every `Rent Income` head — which is already inside sales revenue — to
   "other income". `other_income` now carries only income from outside the sales
   register (`other_expenses` / typed income payments). Rs 8 695.04 was double
   counted for this workbook.
7. **Bank rows carried dates that hid a double count.** The workbook dates for
   `QR-012`, `QR-044` and `QR-082` differ from what was stored, so the same
   voucher sat in `petty_cash` (workbook date) and in `bank_transactions` (later
   date); the “same date + amount + payee” dedupe could not pair them and both
   were expensed. Corrections applied from the workbook:

   | Ref | Stored | Workbook | Effect |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | `QR-012` | 2083-04-08 | 2083-03-31 | electricity 12 010 counted once |
   | `QR-044` | 2083-05-06 | 2083-04-31 | water 2 278 counted once |
   | `QR-082` | 2083-06-05 | 2083-05-31 | electricity 71 825 counted once |
   | `QR-056` | OFFICE EXPENSES | INVESTMENT | capital, not an expense |
   | `QR-078` | credit 4 500 | credit 4 510 | receipt matches the sheet |

8. **Employee "delete" was not audited.** It deactivates the row (history stays),
   which is right, but nothing was written to `audit_log`. It now logs the
   deactivation.
