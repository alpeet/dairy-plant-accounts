Formula reference

Mass balance is applied on the same unit throughout; percentages are by mass.

StepFormulaNotes
Cream separationWc = W × (F1 − F2) ÷ (Fc − F2)Cream removed at Fc% fat; remaining milk = W − Wc
Cream additionWc = W × (F2 − F1) ÷ (Fc − F2)Final quantity = W + Wc
Skim milk additionWs = W × (F1 − F2) ÷ (F2 − Fs)Final quantity = W + Ws
Water additionWw = W × (F1 − F2) ÷ F2Dilutes SNF — normally not permitted
SNF after operationSNF = (W × SNF1 ± Wx × SNFx) ÷ (W ± Wx)Cream ≈ 2%, skim ≈ 9%, water 0% (editable)
SMP reconstitutionSMP = Wt × (SNFt − SNFc) ÷ (SMP_SNF − SNFt)SMP SNF ≈ 96% (editable)
Richmond (SNF from CLR)SNF% = CLR ÷ 4 + 0.2 × Fat% + 0.14CLR corrected at 27 °C
